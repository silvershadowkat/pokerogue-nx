The versioned shared-base/Debug installer remains ours. Downloaded packages are

computed by a sequential read after download; this differs from Vita's streaming
implementation. We deliberately do not import its option to delete a working
archive before an update, since that archive may also be the pinned Debug base.

release tags cannot publish a Vita package accidentally.
